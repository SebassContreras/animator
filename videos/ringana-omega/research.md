## Myths and claims not to use
- "Prevents/treats heart disease", "anti-inflammatory", "fights depression", "extends life": P2's background mentions these as research areas, but they are not authorised claims. The US label says the product is "not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease".
- "Free of heavy metals": not stated on P1.
- Triglycerides (2 g/day) and blood pressure (3 g/day): the product provides 960 mg, below those doses.
- Don't present F12 as independent science; say "RINGANA study".
